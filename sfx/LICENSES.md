Kenney has no pack labelled "whoosh"; the whooshes are the Sci-Fi Sounds door
swishes (filtered noise sweeps) and an Interface Sounds swish. Clips were picked
by measured envelope and spectrum, not by ear. `manifest.json` lists each clip's
category, duration and peak time (used to land a whoosh's peak on the cut).
